One Pan Chicken Sausage Gnocchi Veggie Bake

Originally from @katrinamckenna_ — with broccoli & Brussels sprouts

Servings: 4  ·  Source: Instagram @annalee15, March 12, 2026

Ingredients

  • 1 pack chicken sausage (4 links; Aidells Chicken & Apple works well)
  • 1 bag or head of broccoli
  • 1 bag halved Brussels sprouts
  • 1 jar store-bought pesto (about 2 big scoops)
  • Olive oil
  • 1 package gnocchi (regular or mini)
  • Parmesan and/or balsamic glaze, for topping

Quick steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Slice the sausage and add it to a large baking pan.
  3. Add the broccoli, Brussels sprouts, and gnocchi.
  4. Toss everything with 2 big scoops of pesto, plus a little olive oil if it looks dry.
  5. Bake 30 minutes.
  6. Top with Parmesan and a drizzle of balsamic glaze.
Detailed instructions
  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Cut the chicken sausage into small slices and add them to a large baking pan.
  3. Add one bag (or head) of broccoli and one bag of halved Brussels sprouts to the pan — use as much as you like.
  4. Add one package of gnocchi straight to the pan; mini gnocchi works fine too.
  5. Add about two big scoops of pesto and toss everything until coated. If things look dry, mix in a small drizzle of olive oil.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes.
  7. Top with grated Parmesan and, if you like, a drizzle of balsamic glaze.

Notes

  • Shelf-stable vacuum-packed gnocchi goes straight on the pan — no boiling needed.
  • Use a large pan so things roast instead of steam; two pans if it's crowded.
