Fearless Chicken Tikka Masala

Servings: 8  ·  Source: The Lazy Genius

Per serving (chicken + sauce only, rice not included)

~325Calories
~27 gProtein
~17 gFat
~12 gCarbs

Estimates based on standard nutrition data — treat as approximate.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp fat (butter and olive oil together, or any combo)
  • 1/3 cup aromatics (equal parts minced garlic and ginger, plus half a minced jalapeño)
  • 1/4 cup store-bought garam masala
  • 2–4 tbsp tomato paste
  • 6–8 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, diced into bite-sized chunks
  • Four 15-oz cans of tomatoes (a mix of diced and crushed/plain sauce, to taste)
  • A generous glug of heavy cream
  • Jasmine rice, for serving
  • Cilantro, for topping
  • Salt

Quick steps

  1. Heat the fat in a pot over medium-high heat.
  2. Add aromatics, stir ~10 seconds.
  3. Add garam masala, stir ~5–10 seconds.
  4. Stir in tomato paste for a few seconds.
  5. Add salted, diced chicken and stir to coat.
  6. Add canned tomatoes and heavy cream, plus a few pinches of salt.
  7. Simmer ~20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Taste and adjust salt.
  9. Serve over jasmine rice, topped with cilantro.
Detailed instructions
  1. Gather all ingredients before starting — chop aromatics, dice chicken, open cans. This comes together quickly once you start.
  2. Use a wide, heavy-bottomed pot if you have one (shallow Dutch oven or braiser is ideal), though any pot works.
  3. Put the pot over medium-high heat and add the fat. Let it melt and just start to bubble.
  4. Add the aromatics and stir constantly for about 10 seconds — garlic burns fast.
  5. Add the garam masala and stir for another 5–10 seconds, almost frying the spices. Add a splash more fat if the pan looks dry.
  6. Stir in the tomato paste for a few more seconds until it forms a thick, fragrant paste.
  7. Add the diced chicken (seasoned with salt beforehand) and stir until fully coated in the spice paste.
  8. Pour in the canned tomatoes and heavy cream, stir well, and add a few generous pinches of salt. Bring to a gentle simmer — not a hard boil — for about 20 minutes, stirring once or twice. A lid can go over part of the pot if you like.
  9. Taste and add more salt if it needs it.
  10. Serve over jasmine rice, topped with cilantro.

Notes

  • Chicken thighs are forgiving here and hard to overcook — no need to be precise about size.
  • The sauce (minus chicken) can be made ahead and refrigerated; add raw chicken and simmer ~10 minutes when ready to serve.
  • The whole dish also freezes and reheats well.
