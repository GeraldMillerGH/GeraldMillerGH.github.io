Bacon Wrapped Hot Dogs

Oven, grill, or air fryer

Servings: 6  ·  Source: https://www.eatingonadime.com/bacon-wrapped-hot-dogs/

Ingredients

  • 6 hot dogs
  • 6 slices bacon (thin-cut works best)
  • 6 hot dog buns
  • Toothpicks

Quick steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with foil or parchment.
  2. Wrap each hot dog in a slice of bacon and pin both ends with toothpicks.
  3. Bake 13–16 minutes, flipping halfway, until the bacon is crispy.
  4. Rest 5 minutes, pull the toothpicks, and serve in buns.
Detailed instructions
  1. Wrap each hot dog in a slice of bacon, spiraling it so it overlaps as little as possible — that helps it cook evenly. Secure both ends with toothpicks.
  2. Oven: Heat to 400°F and line a baking sheet with foil or parchment. Lay the hot dogs on the sheet and bake 13–16 minutes, flipping halfway, until the bacon is browned and crispy.
  3. Grill: Heat to 400°F and oil the grates or spray them with nonstick spray. Grill with the lid closed 8–12 minutes, turning every 2–3 minutes, until the bacon is crispy.
  4. Air fryer: Heat to 400°F (if there's no preheat setting, run it empty for 5 minutes). Lightly oil the basket and cook in batches if needed: 8 minutes, flip, then 5–6 more minutes until the bacon is crispy.
  5. Whichever method you use, let them rest 5 minutes, remove the toothpicks, and serve in buns.

Notes

  • Thick-cut bacon takes longer to crisp — thin-cut is easier.
  • Polish sausages, beef or pork dogs, or brats all work; turkey bacon is fine too.
  • For a cheesy version, tuck a slice of cheese under the bacon before wrapping.
  • Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge up to 5 days.
