Protein Slow Cooker Sticky Chicken Noodles

Shared by @scaseyfitness, originally from @boredoflunch

Servings: 2  ·  Source: Instagram @scaseyfitness, October 8, 2026

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken breast
  • 20 g dark soy sauce
  • 15 g sriracha
  • 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
  • 20 g honey
  • 25 g garlic paste
  • 8 g ginger paste
  • 100 ml chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch) mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • 200 g protein noodles

Quick steps

  1. Put the chicken breasts in the slow cooker.
  2. Mix soy, sriracha, hoisin, honey, garlic, ginger and stock; pour over the chicken.
  3. Cook on low 4 hours or high 2–3 hours.
  4. Shred the chicken in the pot with two forks.
  5. Stir in the cornflour slurry; cook 15–20 minutes more until sticky and thick.
  6. Cook the noodles, toss them through the chicken and sauce, and serve.
Detailed instructions
  1. Lay the chicken breasts flat in the bottom of the slow cooker.
  2. In a jug, whisk together the dark soy sauce, sriracha, hoisin, honey, garlic paste, ginger paste and chicken stock until the honey dissolves. Pour it over the chicken and turn the pieces to coat.
  3. Put the lid on and cook on low for about 4 hours, or on high for 2–3 hours, until the chicken is cooked through and pulls apart easily.
  4. Shred the chicken right in the pot with two forks and stir it back into the sauce.
  5. Mix the cornflour with the water until smooth, stir it into the pot, and cook with the lid off (or on high) for another 15–20 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the sauce turns thick and glossy.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the protein noodles according to the package. Drain, add them to the slow cooker and toss until everything is coated. Split into two bowls.

Notes

  • The original post lists ingredients only (the method is in the video), so the steps above follow the standard slow-cooker approach for this style of recipe.
  • Top with sliced spring onion, sesame seeds or a squeeze of lime if you like.
  • Keeps well for meal prep — store chicken and noodles together in the fridge for up to 3 days.
