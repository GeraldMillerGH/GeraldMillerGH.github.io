Protein Cookie Dough Balls

Servings: 9 balls (1 ball per serving)  ·  Source: Instagram @juliadaey, July 16, 2024

Per serving (1 ball)

66Calories
4.2 gProtein
3.9 gFat
2.8 gCarbs

As stated in the original post.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz (50 g) peanut butter
  • 2 oz (50 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/4 cup (30 g) almond flour
  • 20 g chocolate chips

Quick steps

  1. Mix all ingredients together.
  2. Shape the dough into 9 small balls.
  3. No baking required.
Detailed instructions
  1. Combine the peanut butter, Greek yogurt, honey, salt, almond flour, and chocolate chips in a bowl.
  2. Mix until fully combined into a dough.
  3. Divide and roll into 9 small balls.
  4. Store in the fridge — no baking needed.
