Salsa Chicken

Instant Pot shredded chicken for tacos and burritos

Servings: 4  ·  Source: https://www.skinnytaste.com/instant-pot-pressure-cooker-easy-salsa/

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast
  • 3/4 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Pepper, to taste
  • Pinch of oregano
  • 1 cup chunky salsa

Quick steps

  1. Season chicken with cumin, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  2. Place in the Instant Pot with the salsa.
  3. Cover and pressure cook 20 minutes.
  4. Let the pressure release, then shred with a fork.
Detailed instructions
  1. Season the chicken breast all over with the cumin, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  2. Place the chicken in the Instant Pot and pour the salsa over it.
  3. Lock the lid and cook on high pressure for 20 minutes.
  4. When the time is up, let the pot release its pressure on its own before opening.
  5. Shred the chicken with two forks right in the pot and stir it into the salsa.

Notes

  • Great for tacos and burritos.
