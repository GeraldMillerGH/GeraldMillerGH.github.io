Jasmine Rice

Stovetop, about 30 minutes

Servings: 4 (about 3 cups cooked)  ·  Source: Gerald's Meal Rotation

Ingredients

  • 1 cup jasmine rice
  • 1 3/4 cups water
  • Pinch of salt
  • Pat of butter

Quick steps

  1. Put rice, water, salt, and butter in your smallest stovetop pot.
  2. Bring to a boil on high.
  3. Cover and turn to low for 15 minutes.
  4. Take off the heat and let sit 10 minutes.
Detailed instructions
  1. Add the rice, water, salt, and butter to the smallest pot that fits them on the stove.
  2. Bring it to a boil over high heat.
  3. As soon as it boils, cover the pot and turn the heat down to low. Cook 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
  4. Take the pot off the heat and let it sit, still covered, for 10 minutes.
  5. Fluff with a fork and serve.
