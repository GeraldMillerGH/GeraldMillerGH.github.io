Change Your Life Chicken

Adapted from The Lazy Genius — with broccoli & sweet potato

Servings: 4  ·  Source: The Lazy Genius

Per serving (1 thigh + ~¼ of the vegetables)

~540Calories
~33 gProtein
~34 gFat
~26 gCarbs

Estimated from standard nutrition data, not lab-tested.

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 lb (about 4 cups) broccoli florets
  • 1.5 lb sweet potato, peeled and cut into large bite-sized cubes
  • 2–3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • Salt (more than you think you need) and black pepper

Quick steps

  1. Preheat oven to 500°F. Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Cut sweet potato and broccoli into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Toss vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper on the pan.
  4. Season chicken (including under the skin) with salt and pepper.
  5. Pat chicken skin dry.
  6. Place chicken skin-side up on the vegetables.
  7. Roast 45–50 minutes.
  8. Rest 5 minutes, then serve.
Detailed instructions
  1. Preheat the oven to 500°F. Line a large sheet pan with foil.
  2. Cut the sweet potato into large bite-sized cubes (smaller than the broccoli, since it takes a bit longer to cook). Cut the broccoli into similar-sized florets.
  3. Toss the sweet potato and broccoli on the sheet pan with 1–2 tbsp olive oil, a generous amount of salt, and pepper. Spread into a single, snug layer — cozy but not stacked.
  4. Peel back the skin on each chicken thigh. Season both sides of the meat generously with salt and pepper, then season directly under the skin. Fold the skin back over.
  5. Pat the chicken skin dry with paper towels — this is the key to crispy skin.
  6. Place the chicken skin-side up directly on top of the vegetables.
  7. Roast for 45–50 minutes, until the chicken skin is deeply golden and crispy and the vegetables are tender with some crisped edges. No need to flip or stir.
  8. Let rest 5 minutes, then serve straight off the pan.

Notes

  • Sweet potato and broccoli cook at different rates than the original recipe's carrots/green beans, so keep an eye near the 40-minute mark if your pieces are smaller.
  • Leftovers reheat well in an air fryer.
