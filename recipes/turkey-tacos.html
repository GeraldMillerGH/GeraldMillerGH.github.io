Turkey Tacos

Ground turkey & lentils, ready in about 10 minutes

Servings: 4 (8 tacos, 2 per serving)  ·  Source: Gerald's Meal Rotation

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 1 (12 oz) can lentils
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/4 tsp coriander
  • Salt and pepper
  • 8 taco shells
  • 8 tbsp shredded Mexican-blend cheese
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • Cooking spray

Quick steps

  1. Toast the taco shells.
  2. Heat a skillet to medium-high and spray with cooking spray.
  3. Add turkey, break it up, and brown for 30 seconds.
  4. Add cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper; cook 1–2 minutes until fully browned.
  5. Add tomatoes and lentils; cook ~5 minutes until the juice evaporates.
  6. Fill shells and top with cheese and lettuce.
Detailed instructions
  1. Toast the taco shells first so they're ready when the filling is done.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and coat it with cooking spray.
  3. Add the ground turkey and break it up with a spatula. Let it brown for about 30 seconds.
  4. Sprinkle in the cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper. Keep breaking up the meat and cook 1–2 minutes, until it's fully browned.
  5. Stir in the diced tomatoes and lentils. Cook about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the liquid has evaporated.
  6. Spoon the filling into the shells (about 2 per serving) and top each taco with 1 tbsp shredded cheese and some lettuce.

Notes

  • Pairs well with avocado, guacamole, or hot sauce.
